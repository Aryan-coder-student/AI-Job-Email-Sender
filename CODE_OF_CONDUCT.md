# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Job Send Crawl a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- being respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions;
- giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith;
- focusing criticism on code, design, behavior, and evidence rather than people;
- respecting differing viewpoints and levels of experience;
- protecting other people's privacy and confidential information;
- helping newcomers understand the project without belittling them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal insults, trolling, or sustained hostile communication;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- deliberately disrupting project discussions or contributor workflows;
- abusing issue trackers, pull requests, or other project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other official collaboration channels. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and discussions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

To report conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through an available GitHub contact channel. Do not use a public issue if doing so would expose sensitive information about the people involved.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as reasonably possible. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the needs of this project.
